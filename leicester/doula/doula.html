Doula Services

Nurturing postnatal support

Compassionate, continuous and non-judgemental support through your fourth trimester. Helping you rest, recover and connect as a new family with trained doula Lara Bailey.

Postnatal doula

Care for you, so you can care for your baby

A chance for you to talk through your birth experience, ask any questions you may have, or simply be listened to. I can care for your newborn while you sleep, eat, connect with other children, shower, or take a minute to breathe.

I'm also available to help with light cleaning and preparing nourishing meals while you spend time as a new family, and with feeding support, both breast and bottle.

Tailored, flexible hourly sessions booked around your schedule. Available across Mountsorrel, Rothley and surrounding Leicestershire areas.
