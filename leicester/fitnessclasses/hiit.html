Online HIIT

Live 30-minute workouts, streamed to your home

Fast, fun and effective interval workouts streamed live on Zoom. Build strength, boost your fitness and get your heart pumping with an encouraging community, all from your living room.

See classes & dates

One booking, one screen

Anyone at home can join in: on your own, with a partner, or get the whole household involved. The kids might pop in and out too!

Just 30 minutes

Short on time? These sessions deliver maximum energy and results with zero travel time.

Your own pace

Options to make every exercise easier or harder, so it works for your fitness level.

Please note: these classes are not suitable during pregnancy. Have a look at Strong Women Squad instead, which has safe options for pregnant mums.

HIIT Cardio

Saturdays9:30amLive on Zoom

A quick and effective 30-minute cardio workout designed to improve fitness, boost energy, and leave you feeling great (and a bit knackered 😉). Combining bursts of high-intensity movement with rest periods, you'll work at your own pace with options to adjust the intensity to suit you.

No equipment needed. Just bring yourself, make some space and get ready to move!

HIIT Strength

Saturdays9:30amLive on Zoom

Build strength, improve fitness and feel stronger in just 30 minutes! Using light weights and bodyweight exercises, this class combines strength training in interval rounds for an all-round, full-body workout.

Grab your weights (or household alternatives like water bottles or tins), make some space and enjoy your workout from the comfort of home.
