Our Womanhood Offerings

Bring the sparkle back to your weekly routine with our empowering, energizing class styles:

Dance & Tone

Dance & Tone is our fun dance-inspired fitness class to bring the sparkle back to your Monday! We combine a variety of dance styles to top 40s hits — think salsa one minute, ballet-inspired soft leaps the next, straight into twerking and dropping it low. Leave your inhibitions at the door and come along to have fun with our girl gang. You won't even realize you're exercising with those endorphins flowing!

Strong & Stretched

A wonderful combination to finish off your Monday evening. We start our workout with a gentle warm-up, followed by full-body muscle conditioning, and finish with a deep, restorative full-body stretch. Helping build strength without heavy impact, making it gentle on joints and bones while easing aches and pains ready for the week ahead.

Monday Double

Take on the ultimate Monday double challenge! Do both DANCE & TONE (6.30pm) and STRONG & STRETCHED (7.00pm) back-to-back for a discounted bundle price of just £10 for both classes (rather than £7 per class). The perfect high-energy, feel-good start to your week!
