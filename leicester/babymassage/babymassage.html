Why include baby massage?

Baby massage has so many benefits for both you and baby, and here are just a few that you can gain by introducing this within your family:

Bonding & Connection

Baby massage increases oxytocin (the love hormone), which when combined with gentle massage strokes, eye contact, and soothing songs helps nurture an unbreakable bond between parent and baby.

Natural Pain Relief

Helps relieve discomfort from common infant problems such as colic, teething pain, chest congestion, trapped gas, and constipation. It also gently releases muscles tightened in the womb or during birth.

Brain Stimulation & Alertness

Studies show that infant massage improves short- and long-term brain function by multiplying neural connections in baby's brain, promoting healthy development and alertness.

Deep Relaxation & Sleep

Oxytocin converts into serotonin (the sleepy hormone), helping baby settle deeper and sleep longer. We always finish each class with a guided Mummy Relaxation so you can rest your body and clear your mind while baby snuggles up.

When can I join?

We welcome babies from 6–8 weeks old up until pre-crawling. This is often one of the first outings for mum and baby, so be assured of a small, calm group environment, a listening ear, and a safe space. Each course runs for 4 weeks.

What should I bring?

I supply the organic massage oil, squishy mats, cosy blankets, and a calming atmosphere, along with a printed copy of our routine. Just bring whatever you need for yourself or baby (milk, nappies, etc.).
