Meet Lara Bailey

Founder, Fitness Coach & Maternal Specialist

Hi, I'm Lara! I'm 35, wife to Dan (our tech whizz), and mum to our beautiful daughter Luna. I'm a lover of fitness and women's wellness, babies, pregnancies and birth as well as all things celestial, crystals and mystical. I'm always on hand with a supportive word, a listening ear, a shoulder to cry on or a woo at your achievements! Whatever you need, I'm your girl!

You will normally recognise me from the rainbow hair which I've had for about 10 years now. It changes often though, so I'm never one colour for a long time! If you have little ones, I apologise for the questions of how to get those colours that often ensue too!

Fitness Career

I trained to be a fitness instructor when I was 18 and have been helping people on their health journeys ever since! I initially trained in group fitness, quickly going on to qualify in various Les Mills programmes (6 to be exact) where I was selected as a Tribe Coach to support new instructors.

Over the years I also qualified in indoor cycling, Zumba, INSANITY, and aqua aerobics. I completed my Personal Training qualification shortly after and have coached clients 1-to-1 for well over 10 years. I love connecting on a personal level while helping you achieve tangible, empowering results!

Pre & Postnatal Fitness

Despite working in fitness for so long, I didn't specialise in pre and postnatal fitness until having Luna in 2020. Experiencing pregnancy and an emergency Caesarean recovery gave me first-hand insight into what women truly feel and need during this transformative time.

I trained throughout my pregnancy right up until 36 weeks and coached my last PT client 4 days before my due date! Keeping my fitness up supported a healthy pregnancy and smoother recovery. I am fully qualified to programme safely for every trimester and fourth-trimester recovery with the latest medical guidance.

Supporting Motherhood

I qualified as a baby massage instructor to help parents and infants bond deeply through soothing touch, songs, and eye contact. Seeing how massage eases infant colic, promotes deeper sleep, and provides a calm, safe sanctuary for mothers after birth inspired me to bring this to Leicestershire.

In 2022, I completed Doula training with Every Birth Matters alongside hypnobirthing skills. I believe deeply in giving women continuous, non-judgemental guidance and practical hands-on care as they transition into life with a newborn.

Inclusivity

I have a couple of autoimmune conditions under my belt as well as a bowel condition. So if you are attending with some of these too, I feel you and understand. I'll always make sure you are checking in with your body too, after all you know it best! We have also lost two pregnancies in 2023 and 2024, so if you are joining my motherhood journey classes and are having some anxiety after loss or during a fertility journey, I can fully empathise and will always have a listening ear.
