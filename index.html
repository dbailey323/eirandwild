    Good to know

    Common questions

    Can I bring my baby to the classes?

    Yes! That is the heart of Eir & Wild. Pre-crawlers can relax on a mat or cosy blanket right next to you. If your little one is on the move, they can relax in a pushchair or carrier while you enjoy your workout.

    When can I start exercising again after birth?

    A minimum of 6 weeks after a vaginal birth, or 12 weeks after a caesarean, and always after clearance from your GP or healthcare professional at your 6–8 week postnatal check.

    When can my baby start baby massage?

    Babies are welcome from 6–8 weeks old up until they start crawling. Each course runs for 4 weeks.
